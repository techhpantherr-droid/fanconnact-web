Prediction Arena

How To Play

Predict smart. Earn XP. Collect Coins. Climb the Leaderboard.

1 How predictions work

  • Choose an available prediction for the selected cricket match.
  • Select one option and submit your prediction.
  • Once submitted, your prediction cannot be changed.
  • Predictions appear only when their rule/trigger is active; the system does not create questions on every over.

2 Pre-match predictions

  • Match Winner: Who will win the match?
  • Toss: Who will win the toss?
  • Highest Run Scorer: Who will be the highest run scorer?
  • Total Score: What will be the total score?

Only a small number of pre-match questions are kept active at a time.

3 Live match predictions

  • Next Over Runs: Predict the runs in the selected over checkpoint.
  • Powerplay Score: Predict the score during the powerplay window.
  • Next Wicket: Predict which bowler will take the next wicket.
  • Next Boundary: Predict whether the next scoring shot will be a boundary.
  • Next Six: Predict whether a six will be hit in the next over.
  • Wicket: Predict whether a wicket will fall in the next over when that trigger is available.

4 Special live predictions

  • Next Ball Event: After a real Four, Six, or Wicket event, predict what happens on the next ball: Four, Six, Wicket, or Other.
  • Fifty: When the current batter is approaching 50, predict whether they will score a Fifty.
  • Century: When the current batter is approaching 100, predict whether they will score a Century.
  • Death Over: During the configured death-over point, predict the runs scored in that over.
  • Chase: In a close late chase, predict whether the batting team will successfully chase the target.

5 Cricket format rules

  • The Prediction Arena is cricket-only right now.
  • The system recognizes T10, T20, ODI, The Hundred, and Test formats.
  • Normal-over questions use selected checkpoints instead of appearing every over.
  • Powerplay and death-over questions follow the format's configured windows.
  • Live prediction limits are format-specific, so the board does not become overloaded with questions.

6 Results & rewards

  • Live/event predictions can be resolved during the match when the required result is available.
  • Match Winner, Highest Scorer, and Total Score are final-outcome predictions and are resolved only after the match is actually finished.
  • Correctness is checked against the actual match result/event data.
  • Rewards are processed from your selected option, prediction difficulty, and whether the prediction was correct.
  • Your completed predictions move to Results/My Predictions after they are resolved.
